Finding 1 — SGC needs cross-type coupling badly: removing it roughly halves SGC's accuracy on every dataset (64.94→37, 91.95→50, 91.99→31), while GCN/GCN2 lose only 2–8 points. Sum-aggregation appears far more dependent on cross-type structure than mean-aggregation.
Finding 2 — 4.3.1 and 4.3.2 are mathematically equivalent on ACM/DBLP (bit-identical results), because the coarsened adjacency and balance term are provably invariant to the mu-normalization difference between them in this regime. They are not independent evidence on these two datasets — a caveat for the write-up.
Finding 3 — Laplacian smoothness ties or beats full GW-based BEFGC at r=0.3 for node classification, on all three datasets. The extra sophistication of Gromov-Wasserstein isn't clearly earning its keep here (see §3 — same pattern holds for link prediction too).

5Ratio Sweep (r = 0.15 / 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.5) — Complete

Full sweep across all 4 methods (main BEFGC + 3 ablations) × 3 datasets × 4 ratios — 36 combinations, node-classification accuracy. Condensed to key takeaways below; full 36-cell table in ICLR_RESULTS.md.

SGC's cross-type dependency is ratio-independent: ablating cross-type coupling collapses SGC to ~37% (IMDB), ~50% (ACM), ~31% (DBLP) at every ratio tested — this is a structural property, not something more or less coarsening fixes.
GW≈Laplacian holds across the whole ratio sweep, not just r=0.3 — confirmed at r=0.15, 0.4, and 0.5 too.
DBLP is completely clean at every ratio, for every method, in every experiment run this session (std always ≤1.63) — a useful negative control showing the ACM+GCN2 finding below is dataset-specific, not a general artifact.

6Key New Finding: ACM+GCN2 Instability

The most significant new discovery this session — a genuine, recurring instability, not a fluke, not tied to one ablation.

Bimodal split at r=0.15 (full unmodified BEFGC, 10-seed rerun): 82.71±5.50 — not an outlier, a real ~50/50 split. 5 seeds land at 87-89%, the other 5 at 75-79%. GCN shows the same pattern more mildly.
Catastrophic single-seed collapse at r=0.4 (full unmodified BEFGC): one seed drops to 58.0% (near majority-class) while four others hold at 93-95% (std=14.41) — a different failure character than r=0.15, but still real.
Clean at r=0.5 for both the full method (95.20±0.45) and Laplacian (93.98±1.35) — confined to smaller/mid ratios.
Laplacian ablation shows the most severe version: 76.19±14.85 (r=0.15), 85.98±12.63 (r=0.3), 76.63±16.17 (r=0.4) — the ablation amplifies a pre-existing weak spot, it doesn't create it.
Confirmed in a totally unrelated context: LastFM link prediction (no-cross-type ablation, user-user relation) shows GCN2 at 72.72±8.41 with 2 of 5 seeds collapsed — different dataset, different ablation, different task, same GCN2-specific pattern.
Recommendation: mean±std over 5 seeds is misleading for ACM+GCN2 specifically — it can average a genuine bimodal split into a number that looks like moderate noise. A median or best-of-k-via-validation selection would report this cell more honestly, and the underlying cause (GCN2's GCNII-style skip connection interacting with ACM's coarsening?) deserves its own investigation rather than being averaged away.
